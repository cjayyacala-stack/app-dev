# My Favorite Series — *Peaky Blinders*

Title: Peaky Blinders  
Genre:Crime / Drama / Historical  
Seasons watched: 6

## Why I like it
- The concept of **gangs** and power dynamics is really interesting.  
- Intense storyline with deep character development.  
- Stunning cinematography and period setting after World War I.  
- Powerful performances, especially by Cillian Murphy as Thomas Shelby.  
- The soundtrack perfectly fits the dark and stylish tone of the show.

### Favorite characters
1. **Thomas Shelby** — a brilliant and ruthless leader.  
2. **Arthur Shelby** — loyal but unpredictable older brother.  
3. **Polly Gray** — the strong and wise matriarch of the family.

> “You can change what you do, but you can’t change what you want.” — *Thomas Shelby*

## Memorable scenes
- The razor blade reveal in the first episode.  
- The Shelby family taking over Birmingham.  
- Tommy’s strategic political moves in later seasons.
